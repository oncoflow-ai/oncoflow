Right Fixed Sidebar

Clinical data (stats, chart, history, AI insights) scroll in the main column. MRI workspace is a fixed right panel (~35% width). Always visible — physician never loses sight of the viewer.

Tabbed Layout

Two tabs: "Clinical Overview" (stats, chart, history, AI) and "MRI Workspace" (viewer + tools). Keeps each context focused. Best for small screens. Less useful when physician wants data + viewer simultaneously.
